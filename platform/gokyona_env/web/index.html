Clades by descent (founder tags): top clade share, both halves

Clade sweep: every chain gets a founder tag at each census (default every 100 simulated years); copies and fragments inherit it. A clade = all chains carrying one tag. An event is logged when one tag first reaches ≥ 30 % of its half's tagged chains (both halves). A real-half event counts only if (1) share ≥ 30 %, (2) share > the 95th percentile of the control half's top-clade share over all reports so far (≥ 20 control reports, else "pending"), and (3) the clade's mean z > the real half's mean z at its census (a fitter clade, not drift). Sustained evolution also = ≥ 2 counted clade sweeps.
